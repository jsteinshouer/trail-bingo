The Card, as a map sheet

The on-trail home screen in the Topo Quadrangle direction. A new Card is a bare map: names and legend symbols, no photos. Spotting the thing is the game. Each Sighting prints the player's own photo onto the sheet, so a finished Card is a record of the hike.

Card size
Try it

Tap any Square to open its detail. Unmarked Squares offer a clue; marked ones show the Sighting photo. The orange "Mockup only" box in the detail stands in for ticket 02's dev control. Take a Sighting marks the next open Square.

Legend

Unmarked. The woodland tint with contours, a legend symbol for the kind of thing, and the name. No photo. On 3 × 3 and 4 × 4 the scientific name sits below in brown italic.
Verified. The player's Sighting photo develops onto the Square, with a solid purple name strip and a crisp printed check. The photo check was sure.
Confirmed. The Sighting photo with a hatched purple strip and a hand-pencilled tick. The player chose from the top guesses. Same ink as Verified because it counts the same; only the mark differs.
Wildcard. A triangulation-station mark on open paper. Once filled, it shows the Sighting photo and names what filled it.
Bingo. The map's trail symbol is drawn through the line and stays on the Card.
  • Tree or shrub
  • Plant or wildflower
  • Fungus or lichen
  • Animal

Clues

  • Tapping an unmarked Square opens its detail: name, scientific name, kind, and a Show a clue button. The clue reveals the reference photo, with its credit line right beneath it.
  • Animal Squares show a representative local species as the clue ("for example, a white-tailed deer").
  • Clue photos are still downloaded when the Card is built, so clues work offline.

Screen anatomy

  • Top collar: the place in large condensed caps, as on a quad's title block, with state, month and the Ready offline line beside it. The grid icon opens the Card menu (new Card, photo credits). The app name sits in the bottom margin, where a quad prints its publisher.
  • The sheet: a neatline frame with corner coordinates, tick marks at every Square boundary, and one continuous contour field running under every unmarked Square. As Sightings land, photos cover more of the map.
  • Bottom collar: the scale bar is the progress meter, one segment per Square, filled purple as Squares are marked. The Bingo count sits at the right.
  • Take a Sighting: a full-width purple band at the thumb. It's the only filled control on the screen.

Fitting 5 × 5

  • The whole Card is always on one screen, at every size. No scrolling or panning.
  • Without reference photos, names get more room: three lines of 13 px condensed semibold at 5 × 5, two lines at 4 × 4 and 3 × 3 with the scientific name below. Names are bottom-aligned in every state, so rows read evenly.

Celebrations

  • Bingo: the Sighting photo develops, then the trail line draws across the completed line, the sheet gives a small shake with a navigator.vibrate([40, 60, 40]) pulse, and a purple revision stamp says BINGO for about 2.6 s, then clears on its own. No sound.
  • Blackout: a larger stamp over the Card, which is now all photos. It stays until tapped.
  • Reduced motion: no developing, drawing or scaling. The stamp shows and hides without animating.

Tokens

  • Inks: paper #fdfdfb, woodland #d3e8c1, contour #9c5a26 / #7a4216, ink #1b1b1a, photorevision purple #7b3294 / #5c2170. Hydrography blue #1f6fb0 is used only for focus rings.
  • Type: Barlow Condensed for map lettering, Barlow for running text and scientific names.
  • No radius, no card chrome, light only.
