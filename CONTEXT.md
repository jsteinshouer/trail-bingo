# Trail Bingo

A bingo game played on a hike: players spot living things from their card out in the world and prove each sighting with a photo.

## Language

**Card**:
A bingo grid of Squares built for one place and season from species actually observed nearby.
_Avoid_: Board, sheet

**Square**:
One cell on a Card naming what to find: a single species for plants, trees and fungi, or a broad group for animals (e.g. "a mammal", "a reptile").
_Avoid_: Tile, cell, item

**Wildcard**:
A special Square that any living thing the photo check recognizes can fill, whether or not it's on the Card.
_Avoid_: Free space, joker

**Sighting**:
A photo the player takes on the trail to claim a Square.
_Avoid_: Capture, find, observation

**Verified**:
A Square marked because the photo check confidently matched a Sighting to it.

**Confirmed**:
A Square marked because the player chose it from the photo check's top guesses when the check wasn't sure. Counts toward bingo the same as Verified.
_Avoid_: Self-reported, manual

**Bingo**:
A full row, column or diagonal of marked Squares. Getting one doesn't end the game.
_Avoid_: Win, line

**Blackout**:
Every Square on a Card marked.
_Avoid_: Full card, coverall
